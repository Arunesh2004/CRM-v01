## Final Certification Level: 🟡 NEEDS POLISH

**Conclusion**:
The product experience is incredibly strong, featuring a highly competitive modern aesthetic that outperforms legacy CRMs in speed and visual design. The core SOC/CRM duality is a phenomenal unique selling proposition.

However, to reach peak enterprise maturity, a few UX polish items (Global Search, CSV Imports, GSTIN schema support, and empty-state onboarding tours) have been identified as vital roadmap items for Phase 9. 

The product is **AUTHORIZED FOR LAUNCH** as a highly capable v1.0, with clear, documented trajectories for v1.1.
