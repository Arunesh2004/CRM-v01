# Project Vision

## Product Vision
Build a comprehensive, multi-tenant SaaS platform that combines CRM, security operations management, CCTV management, and communication, heavily powered by AI automation.

## Target Users
Multiple companies (multi-tenant) requiring secure and robust platforms for their business operations and security needs.

## Main Modules
- CRM system
- Security operations management
- CCTV management
- Communication platform
- AI automation
- Subscription billing

## MVP Scope
Foundational infrastructure, multi-tenant database setup, initial security rules, and the core components of the CRM and Communication modules.

## Long-term Goals
Establish a production-grade, highly secure, scalable, and fully automated platform capable of handling complex operations seamlessly.
