# PHASE 8.14 DAILY WORKFLOW REVIEW

## Objective
Evaluate friction points in a standard Sales and Operations daily routine.

## Simulation Routine

1. **Morning (Review)**:
   - Dashboard loads instantly.
   - Pending Tasks are clearly visible.
   - The Leads pipeline gives a good 10,000-foot view.

2. **Afternoon (Action)**:
   - Adding a new Customer requires navigating to `/customers`, clicking "Add Customer", and filling the modal. 
   - *Friction*: There is no "Global Add" (+) button in the header. To add a lead while looking at the Dashboard requires 2 extra clicks.
   - Updating lead status via Kanban drag-and-drop is exceptionally satisfying and frictionless.

3. **Evening (Reporting)**:
   - The Reports tab clearly delineates Leads Won vs Lost.
   - The SOC Incident Dashboard is completely segregated from Sales flows, ensuring Security Operators are not distracted by sales noise.

## Status
**YELLOW (NEEDS POLISH)**
The core flows are rock solid, but introducing a global "Quick Add" shortcut (e.g., `Cmd + K` or a Header `+` Button) would vastly accelerate daily power-user workflows.
